Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to Sunrise Staffing Solution. Learn how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last Updated: 21 August 2026

1. Introduction

Sunrise Staffing Solution Private Limited ("Sunrise", "we", "us" or "our") respects the privacy of individuals who visit our website or contact us through our online channels.

This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through this website may be collected, used and protected.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may receive information such as:

  • Full name
  • Company or organization name
  • Telephone or mobile number
  • Email address
  • Service or business requirement
  • Information included in your enquiry or message

3. How We Use Information

Information submitted through our website may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests.
  • Understand your facility management or workforce requirements.
  • Provide information about our services.
  • Contact you regarding an enquiry or requested service.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Maintain website security and prevent misuse.

4. Enquiry Information

When you submit an enquiry through our website, the information you provide may be used by Sunrise personnel to respond to your request.

Our current website enquiry process may redirect the information you provide to WhatsApp for communication with Sunrise.

5. Sharing of Information

Sunrise does not intend to sell personal information submitted through this website.

Information may be shared with service providers or other parties where reasonably necessary to operate the website, respond to enquiries, provide requested services, comply with applicable law or protect the rights and security of Sunrise.

6. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services, such as Google Maps, web hosting services, analytics tools or communication platforms.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Cookies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies where required for functionality, security, analytics or improving the user experience.

Any future use of cookies for analytics, advertising or other purposes should be reflected in an updated version of this Privacy Policy where applicable.

8. Data Security

Sunrise takes reasonable measures to protect information received through the website against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to respond to enquiries, maintain business records, comply with applicable legal obligations or resolve disputes.

10. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights relating to their personal data, including rights to obtain information about processing, correction, withdrawal of consent where applicable and grievance redressal.

To make a privacy-related request, contact Sunrise using the details below.
