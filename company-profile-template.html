Company Profile | Executive Overview
Corporate Heritage & Capability

Pioneering Integrated Facility Excellence

A heritage of dependable service delivery, rigorous statutory transparency, and strategic enterprise partnerships across India's premier industrial corridors.

SUNRISE STAFFING SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD. was established in 2006 under the visionary stewardship of Mr. Kiran Vishnu Panchpute. Founded in Pune, Maharashtra, the organization emerged to bridge a vital industrial need: delivering dependable, high-caliber integrated facility management and skilled workforce solutions that allow manufacturing leaders to concentrate entirely on their core production goals.

Over nearly two decades of uninterrupted operations, Sunrise has evolved from a boutique labor contractor into a premier integrated facility management partner. Today, Sunrise manages complex manufacturing campuses, automotive assembly lines, IT parks, logistics hubs, and executive guest facilities.

Our operational philosophy rests on an uncompromising foundation of 100% statutory adherence, proactive supervision, rigorous workforce training, and continuous investment in human capital.
